About

Zuhlume Sigwabe aka Keen, is a South African musician that grew up in a small town in Eastern Cape called KinG Willaims Town. At a young stage Keen fell inlove with music and decided to persue on this music journey. Keen music is rooted in hip/hop,Rnb and trap. Keen is also part of the crew/ Record Label named the Savage kings which is composed of his close friends. New Horizons is the newest chapter, out now on every major platform.
